Samsung Tizen and LG webOS TVs pick up the dashboard's remote capture gesture (by default a 1.5 s press of Play/Pause) to link the TV and file quick captures. A linked TV can also record a new binding from the remote. Only linked TVs swallow the gesture's keys, and the SDK registers Tizen keys only on linked TVs and debug builds.
