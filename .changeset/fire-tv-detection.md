Fire TV devices are recognized as TVs by Amazon's `amazon.hardware.fire_tv` system feature as well as by the television UI mode and the leanback feature. The remote capture gesture, the `tv` form factor and remote breadcrumbs therefore apply on a Fire TV even when it reports neither of the Android TV signals. Not verified on Fire TV hardware yet.
